Core features

Simple, polished, and practical. My Work Shift Planner focuses on the tools people actually need to plan shifts, follow hours, and stay organized.

Shift calendar Review a whole month clearly with shift symbols, selected-day detail, and upcoming work always visible.
Quick multi-day adding Set a shift once and place it across multiple dates faster when building out a month.
Rotation presets Apply recurring work patterns faster and keep future months more organized with less manual editing.
Work shift types Plan day, evening, night, overtime, standby, on-call, split, double, training, meeting, leave, and more.
Daily shift detail Open any day to review scheduled shifts, timing, notes, workplace details, and related tags.
Estimated pay Review monthly pay estimates using user-set hourly rate, differentials, overtime rules, and bonuses.
Work profiles Manage multiple workplaces or contracts in one planner and assign shifts to the correct profile.
Shift reminders Get local reminders before upcoming work so important shifts are harder to miss.
CSV and PDF exports Export monthly and yearly records in clean formats that are easier to save, review, or share.
Private by design Use fast local storage by default with optional private iCloud sync when enabled.
