How our content is made

Editorial policy and transparency

We publish practical content to help people understand and organize shift work. This policy explains who creates it, how it is checked, what sources are preferred and where its limits apply.

Last reviewed August 1, 2026

Authorship and review

Paul Crapatureanu writes or reviews every page and checks product references against current versions of the apps. Guides display their publication or review date and are revisited after relevant product changes.

Method and sources

We prioritize official documentation, rules published by employers or public authorities, and direct product testing. We avoid presenting a schedule pattern, payroll rule or employment practice as universal when it varies by employer or region.

Calculations and limitations

Calculators and pay estimates are planning tools. They do not automatically account for taxes, collective agreements, local labor law, every employer policy or payroll rounding. Users should verify final figures with official payroll information.

Corrections and updates

If you identify an error, contact contact@paulcrp.com. Reports are reviewed and content is corrected or clarified when appropriate. Substantive updates change the review date.

Commercial relationship

This website promotes apps made by the same independent developer. App Store links and descriptions of subscription features are presented openly. Editorial guides are intended to remain useful even if a reader does not install an app.

Writing and translation tools

Drafting, translation and proofreading tools may assist the process. Every published page is reviewed by the developer for product accuracy, clarity, scope and consistency before it goes live.
