Who is behind the product

Practical tools for schedules that change

My Shift Planner brings together two independent apps built to help people with shift-based work organize their personal calendar, work hours and estimated pay.

Independent development

My Nurse Shift Planner and My Work Shift Planner are independently designed, developed, tested and maintained, together with this website.

Why these apps exist

Shift schedules rarely fit neatly into general calendars. The goal is to provide a focused personal view for rotations, nights, multiple workplaces, hours and estimated pay.

First-hand product experience

Information about app features, workflows and calculations comes from direct implementation and testing. Product pages and guides are reviewed when the apps change.

Privacy as a product decision

The apps use a local-first approach. Optional iCloud sync is provided through Apple services when users choose to enable it; the planner is not an employer scheduling platform.

Important limits

My Shift Planner is not a payroll provider, employer, medical service or legal adviser. Official work times, payroll results and employment rules must be confirmed with the relevant source.

Contact

Questions, corrections and product support can be sent to hello@myshiftplanner.app.
