Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about scheduling, work profiles, reminders, exports, subscriptions, privacy, and how My Nurse Shift Planner works in everyday use.

What is My Nurse Shift Planner for?

It is a personal shift planning app for nurses and healthcare staff. It helps you organize workdays, rotations, reminders, work profiles, estimated pay, and monthly exports in one place.

Who is the app designed for?

The app is designed primarily for nurses, but it can also be useful for other healthcare staff who work rotating schedules, day and night shifts, overtime, or multiple workplaces.

Can I manage more than one workplace?

Yes. You can create multiple work profiles and assign shifts to the correct workplace inside the same calendar.

What shift types can I track?

You can track common nursing shift types such as day, night, evening, on-call, callback, overtime, PTO, sick, education, and cancelled shifts.

Can I apply repeating rotations?

Yes. The app includes rotation presets so repeating work patterns can be applied faster instead of being entered one day at a time.

Can I add the same shift across multiple days quickly?

Yes. Quick multi-day adding lets you configure a shift once and then place it across multiple dates in one session.

Does the app calculate pay?

The app provides estimated pay based on the settings you enter, such as base rate, overtime, differentials, and bonuses. These values are estimates for planning and are not official payroll calculations.

Are reminders automatic?

Reminders work after you enable them and allow notifications on your iPhone. The app schedules local reminders before upcoming shifts based on your selected lead time.

Does the app work without a backend account?

Yes. The app is designed to work locally on your iPhone first, so your planner can load quickly without requiring a separate account system.

Does the app sync with iCloud?

Yes, if you enable iCloud sync. This allows selected app data to sync privately through your Apple account across compatible devices.

Can I export my shifts?

Yes. The app can generate monthly CSV and PDF exports so you can review, save, or share your shift records and summaries.

Are exports and pay summaries official records?

No. Exports and pay summaries are for personal planning and review only. You should always confirm official schedules, payroll statements, and workplace records through your employer's approved systems.

What happens if I travel to a different time zone?

The app keeps the shift record tied to the schedule context you entered and can show local timing information where relevant, helping you understand how shifts appear while traveling.

How do subscriptions work?

Subscriptions are handled through the App Store. Apple manages billing, renewals, and cancellation. You can also use the restore purchases option inside the app if needed.

How can I contact support?

For support, feature questions, or product issues, use the contact options on this website or email hello@myshiftplanner.app.
